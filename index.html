Reminders that follow your day

Link a medicine to breakfast, lunch, dinner, waking or bedtime, or give it a clock time. Move breakfast and everything tied to it moves too.

  • A different dose at each meal, like 14, 8 and 6 units of insulin
  • Weekends can keep their own, later times
  • Every day, some weekdays, every few days, on-and-off cycles or when needed

Log it without opening the app

Each reminder is a real alarm on your phone, on the minute. Tap Taken, Snooze or Skip right on the notification. If nothing's logged, a gentle follow-up comes 30 minutes later.

  • Forgot to tap? Spin the wheel to the time you took it
  • Undo for every action
  • Hide medicine names on the lock screen

Then Pip goes to sleep

Made for real prescriptions

Tend records the plan you were given. It doesn't calculate or recommend doses.

  • Every kind of medicine

    Tablets, capsules, liquids, insulin and other injections, inhalers, drops, creams and patches each get their own fields. No tablet count for a cream.

  • Type it or scan the label

    Write Metformin 500mg 1-0-1 PC or point the camera at the label. Tend fills the form for you to check, and the photo never leaves the phone.

  • Widgets and shortcuts

    See the next dose on your home screen and take or snooze it there. Long-press the icon to log a dose or add a medicine.

  • Travel without the maths

    Tend notices a new time zone and asks whether to follow the local clock or keep your home times.

  • Share and keep

    A medicines card to show a doctor, a calendar file with alerts, and a backup you can restore on any phone.

  • Keeps going when you don't

    Alarms come back after a restart, a clock change or an update. On Xiaomi, Oppo, Vivo, OnePlus and Realme phones, Tend shows where to let it run in the background.

Install Tend

Tend comes straight from its open source code on GitHub rather than the Play Store. Each release is built and signed by GitHub Actions.

  1. Download it on your phone

    Open this page on your Android phone and tap Download. The file is about 21 MB.

  2. Let your browser install it

    Open the download. The first time, Android asks you to allow installs from your browser. Allow it, then tap Install.

  3. Say yes to reminders

    Tend asks for notifications and alarms once, and explains why. To update later, install a newer download over the top. Your medicines stay put.
